### Assistant Response

I have investigated and resolved the delivery accept bug.

### Root Causes Identified
1. **Stale `DeliveryAssignment` Records**: When previous orders were completed or updated through administrative actions, some `DeliveryAssignment` records remained in the database with `status: "assigned"` and `assignedTo` set to the delivery boy's ID.
2. **Single-Record Query & Null Order Trap**: The `/api/delivery/assignment/[id]/accept-assignment` route previously queried `findOne` for active assignments. If it encountered a stale assignment whose order was deleted or missing (`alreadyAssigned.order == null`), `(alreadyAssigned.order as any)?.status` returned `undefined`, causing the backend to falsely flag the delivery boy as busy with `"You are already assigned to another active delivery order."`
3. **Race Condition & Missing Active Delivery Panels**: The frontend `handleAccept` function previously called `fetchCurrentOrder()` before setting local `activeOrder` state, creating a potential race condition where the screen flashed or reset. In addition, the Active Delivery screen was missing dedicated Customer Details and Order Details cards.
